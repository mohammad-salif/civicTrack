# CivicTrack 🏙️

**A Smart Civic Issue Reporting & Resolution Management Platform**

CivicTrack is a smart city platform that connects citizens with government authorities to make civic issue reporting and resolution more transparent, efficient, and accountable.

Citizens can report local problems such as potholes, garbage, damaged streetlights, water leakage, and drainage issues, while government officials can manage complaints, assign tasks, monitor progress, and ensure timely resolution.

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 Citizen Portal
- Report civic issues with descriptions and photo evidence.
- Share issue locations using GPS.
- Track complaint status in real time.
- Explore nearby civic issues.
- Support and verify community-reported problems.
- Receive updates on submitted complaints.

### 🏛️ Government Portal
- Centralized complaint management dashboard.
- Review, prioritize, and assign complaints.
- Manage departments and field officers.
- Monitor complaint progress and deadlines.
- Escalate overdue and high-priority complaints.
- View analytics, reports, and issue locations.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Report** – Citizens submit civic issues with photos and location details.
2. **Verify** – Reports are reviewed and duplicate issues can be identified.
3. **Prioritize** – Issues are categorized based on severity and urgency.
4. **Assign** – Complaints are assigned to the responsible department or officer.
5. **Resolve** – Officials take action and upload resolution evidence.
6. **Track** – Citizens and authorized officials can monitor progress.
